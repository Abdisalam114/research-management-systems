# JUST RMS — Full System Guide (Somali + English)

Authoritative flow: **[SYSTEM_HOW_IT_WORKS.md](./SYSTEM_HOW_IT_WORKS.md)**.

---

## A. System purpose

JUST RMS manages research from **proposal → ethics → peer/committee/(finance) → project → budget → publication**, with separate **Undergraduate** and **Postgraduate** portals.

---

## B. End-to-end journey (grant)

1. Director opens a **Funding Call**.
2. Researcher submits a **Grant fund call** proposal linked to that call (+ ethics).
3. Director **Assign & send to reviewer** → Leadership peer reviews.
4. Director completes peer stage → **Assign & send to committee**.
5. Assigned Coordinator committee decision.
6. Director **Assign & send to finance** → Finance Officer review.
7. Director final **Approve** → **Project** created; budget/grant path continues.
8. Later: progress, closure (Director + Finance), publications.

## C. End-to-end journey (voluntary)

Same as grant **without** finance assign/review. Finance stage is skipped.

---

## D. Somali — Socdaalka

1. Login → dooro portal UG/PG (staff).
2. Proposal submit.
3. Assign peer → peer review.
4. Assign committee → committee decision.
5. Grant: assign finance → finance decision.
6. Approve proposal → project.

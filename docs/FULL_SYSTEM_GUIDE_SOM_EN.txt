1. WHAT IS THIS SYSTEM? / WAA MAXAY SYSTEMKAN?
================================================================================

English: A web app for managing university research from funding call to project closure.

Somali: App web ah oo lagu maamulo cilmi-baarista jaamacadda — laga bilaabo funding call ilaa xiritaanka mashruuca.

English: Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) research share one system. Items are labeled UG or PG.

Somali: UG iyo PG waxay ku jiraan nidaam wadaag ah. Waxyaabaha waxaa lagu calaamadeeyaa UG ama PG.

English: Staff (Director, Coordinator, Finance, Leadership) see UG and PG together — they do not switch portals. Researchers work in their own program tier.

Somali: Shaqaalaha waxay wada arkaan UG iyo PG — ma beddelaan portal. Researchers waxay ku jiraan barnaamijkooda.

2. WHO USES IT? / YAA ISTICMAALA? — SHAQADA MID KASTA
================================================================================

Ethics decisions: Research Director. Peer review: University Leadership. Purchase order review: Finance (before Director). Donor reports: Director (and Finance for financial views).

Research Director
  EN: Sees UG+PG with labels; create/publish funding calls; final proposal approve; ethics; PO after Finance review; donor reports; Users; analytics
  SO: Arag UG+PG; abuuri/daabaca funding calls; ansixinta ugu dambeysa; ethics; PO; donor reports; Users; analytics

University Leadership
  EN: Peer review score 1–5 + comment; KPI — does NOT publish funding calls; sees UG+PG with labels
  SO: Peer review; KPI — MA daabaco funding calls; arag UG+PG

Faculty Coordinator
  EN: Faculty proposal queue; thesis groups; Validate publications → Published; faculty reports; sees UG+PG with labels
  SO: Proposal faculty; thesis; Validate → Published; warbixin faculty; arag UG+PG

Finance Officer — MONEY + PO REVIEW
  EN: Authorize grant budget; review PO before Director; pay budgets/payments; pay PO after Director; finance reports; finance closure; sees UG+PG with labels
  SO: Authorize budget; dib u eegis PO ka hor Director; bixi lacag; bixi PO ka dib Director; reports; xiritaan; arag UG+PG
  EN: Flow: Researcher PO → Finance review → Director → Finance pays
  SO: Safka: Researcher PO → Finance review → Director → Finance bixisaa

Researcher
  EN: Apply, propose, run project, budgets, create PO, publications, thesis, closure (asha@ UG / mahad@ PG)
  SO: Codso, proposal, project, budgets, abuuri PO, publications, thesis, closure (asha@ UG / mahad@ PG)

4. FULL JOURNEY — STEP BY STEP / SAFKA BUUXA
================================================================================

STEP 1 — Funding & Calls / Deeqaha

EN: Director opens Funding Calls and publishes. Researchers apply.
SO: Director wuxuu daabaca Funding Calls. Researchers waxay codsadaan.
Menu: Funding Calls, Grants

STEP 2 — Application (Proposal) / Codsiga

EN — Researcher:
  1. Proposals → New proposal
  2. Fill title, abstract, department, research area
  3. Upload main document
  4. Add budget lines
  5. Upload compliance + supporting docs
  6. Complete Ethics form (JUREC) on the same flow
  7. Save draft → Submit

SO — Researcher: Proposals → cusub → buuxi dhammaan → Submit
Menu: Proposals

STEP 3 — Review & Approval / Dib u eegis & Ansixin

EN order:
  1. Admin screening — Director/Coordinator
  2. Peer review — University Leadership (score + comment)
  3. Committee review
  4. Ethics — Research Director
  5. Finance review — Finance Officer (proposal budget)
  6. Final decision — Director Approve or Reject
  7. If approved → Project created; funding-call grant → pending_finance

SO: Admin → Leadership peer review → Committee → Ethics (Director) → Finance → Director Approve → Project
Menu: Proposals, Peer Reviews, Ethics, Grant funding approval

STEP 4 — Project Management / Maamulka Mashruuca

EN: Projects → timeline, milestones, team, work plan, activities, communication log, progress reports (UG/PG labeled for staff)
SO: Projects → milestones, work plan, hawlaha, communication log, warbixinno
Menu: Projects, Finance & Budgets

STEP 5 — Finance & Purchase Orders / Maaliyad & PO

EN:
  1. Finance authorizes grant budget (Grant funding approval) — allocate only
  2. Researcher uses Budget / payment requests
  3. Purchase orders: Researcher → Finance review → Director → Finance pays

SO:
  1. Finance Authorize budget
  2. Budget / lacag bixin
  3. PO: Researcher → Finance review → Director → Finance

Menu: Grant funding approval, Finance & Budgets, Finance Reports

STEP 6 — Outputs / Natiijooyinka

EN: Publications (1:1 per project). Create & submit → Coordinator/Director Validate = Published. Research Workflow stages, Repository, Thesis (min 4 students), Groups.
SO: Publications → Validate = Published. Repository, Thesis (min 4 arday), Groups.

STEP 7 — Monitoring & Reports / La Socodka

EN: Dashboard, KPI, Finance Reports, Donor Reports, Search, Audit Trail
SO: Dashboard, KPI, Finance Reports, Donor Reports, Search, Audit Trail

STEP 8 — Project Closure / Xiritaanka Mashruuca

EN — Researcher submits checklist + final report + lessons learned → Director → Finance → Archive / Closed
SO: Checklist + final report → Director → Finance → Archive
Menu: Projects → project → Project closure; Finance → Project closure (Finance)

6. SIMPLE PICTURE / SAWIR FUDUD
================================================================================

Funding Call → Grant Application
        ↓
Proposal + Ethics + Budget + Docs
        ↓
Multi-stage Review (Leadership peer review · Director ethics)
        ↓
Director Approves → Project Created · Grant pending_finance
        ↓
Finance Authorize budget → Work Plan + Activities + Budget
        ↓
Payments + Purchase Orders
        ↓

        ↓
Closure Checklist + Lessons Learned
        ↓
Director + Finance Approve → Archived / Closed

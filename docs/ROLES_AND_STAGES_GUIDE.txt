JUST RMS — Roles & Stages
=========================

Portals: Undergraduate | Postgraduate

ROLES
-----
- research_director     Assign peer / committee / finance; ethics certificate; final approve
- faculty_coordinator   Committee review (assigned only)
- leadership            Peer review (assigned only)
- finance_officer       Finance review on grant proposals (assigned); budgets; closure finance
- researcher            Proposals, ethics, projects, publications
- hr / donor            As configured in menus

PROPOSAL STAGES (Phase 3)
-------------------------
1. Peer review          after Assign & send to reviewer
2. Committee            after Assign & send to committee
3. Finance (grant only) after Assign & send to finance
4. Ready for director   Proposal decision unlocked
5. Approved             Project created

Voluntary: finance stage skipped.
Ethics certificate is separate from faculty committee (does not auto-pass committee).

CHIPS ON PROPOSAL LIST
----------------------
Sent to reviewer | Sent to committee | Sent to finance

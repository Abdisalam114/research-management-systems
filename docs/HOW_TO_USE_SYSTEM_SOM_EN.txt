JUST RMS — How to use (English + Somali)
========================================

Full detail: docs/SYSTEM_HOW_IT_WORKS.md

ENGLISH
-------
1. Login. Staff select UG or PG portal.
2. Researcher: create Voluntary or Grant proposal; submit (+ ethics if needed).
3. Director: Assign & send to reviewer → Leadership peer reviews.
4. Director: Assign & send to committee → Coordinator decides (must be assigned).
5. Grant only: Assign & send to finance → Finance decides (must be assigned).
6. When multi-stage is complete: Director Approve proposal → Project created.
7. Finance queue: /finance/reviews (not /proposals/.../review).

SOMALI
------
1. Login. Staff dooro UG ama PG.
2. Researcher: samee Voluntary ama Grant; submit (+ ethics).
3. Director: Assign & send to reviewer.
4. Director: Assign & send to committee.
5. Grant: Assign & send to finance.
6. Phase 3 dhammaato: Approve → Project.
7. Finance: /finance/reviews.

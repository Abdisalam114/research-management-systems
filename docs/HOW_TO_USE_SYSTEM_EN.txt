JUST RMS — How to use (English)
===============================

See docs/SYSTEM_HOW_IT_WORKS.md for the full system description.

1. Login. Staff select Undergraduate or Postgraduate.
2. Researcher submits a Voluntary or Grant fund call proposal (and ethics when required).
3. Director assigns Leadership reviewers (Assign & send to reviewer).
4. After peer review, Director assigns Faculty Coordinators (Assign & send to committee).
5. For grant proposals only, Director assigns Finance (Assign & send to finance).
6. Proposal decision (Approve / Reject) stays locked until multi-stage review is complete.
7. Approve creates a Project. Finance uses /finance/reviews for grant finance review.

JUST RMS — Real example (short)
===============================

Example grant path
------------------
1. Director publishes a funding call.
2. Researcher (UG or PG) submits a grant proposal linked to the call.
3. Director assigns Leadership → they submit peer scores.
4. Director assigns Coordinator → committee recommend approval.
5. Director assigns Finance → finance approve.
6. Director Approve proposal → Project appears under Projects.
7. Budget / payments / closure follow normal finance menus.

Example voluntary path
----------------------
Same without steps 5 (no finance assign/review).
